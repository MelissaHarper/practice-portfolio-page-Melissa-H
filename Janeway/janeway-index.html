About Me

One of the most decorated captains in Starfleet, I grew up in Indiana. My father, a Vice Admiral, was everything I wanted to be. He raised me to never take anything at face value and to look at everything with the eye of a scientist. When I joined Starfleet I learned that, to be a good officer I have to keep an open mind. For my first command, I captained the USS Voyager. On my very first mission, Voyager and the Maqui ship we were following were pulled 70,000 light years across the galaxy. Facing a 70 year journey home, I harnessed the values my father taught me and the lessons I learned at the academy and throughout my career and vowed to my crew that I would get them home.
